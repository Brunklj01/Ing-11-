1What is it?

What is this type of media? What is its main purpose?

An influencer is a person with a large online audience who can affect what their followers like, buy or believe. They post videos, photos and stories on platforms such as Instagram, YouTube and TikTok.

Main purpose: to entertain, inform and inspire, and also to earn money through brand deals, ads and their own products.

2A little bit of history

When did it appear? How has it changed over time?

2000sPersonal blogs and forums create the first online 'stars'.

2005YouTube launches and anyone can become a video creator.

2010Instagram arrives and brands start paying people to show products.

2016–18TikTok and short videos make fame much faster.

2020sInfluencing becomes a real career, called the 'creator economy'.

3How is it used today?

Who uses it? What is it used for? How popular is it?

Who?

People of all ages follow influencers. Brands, charities and even politicians work with them.

What for?

Entertainment, tutorials, reviews, fashion, sport, gaming, news and shopping ideas.

How popular?

Huge. The biggest accounts have hundreds of millions of followers, more than the population of most countries.

4Advantages and disadvantages

Advantages

  • Free entertainment and learning, e.g. a coding or cooking tutorial.
  • Anyone can start a career without a big company.
  • Raises awareness, e.g. MrBeast funding charity projects.

Disadvantages

  • Fake perfect lives can hurt self-esteem.
  • Hidden ads: followers may not notice a post is paid.
  • Fake news, online hate and too much screen time.

5Young people and the media

How do young people use it? How often do they use it?

  • Teenagers watch short videos, follow creators for hobbies, and copy trends, challenges and styles.
  • Most use social media every day, often several times a day, mainly on the phone.
  • Many also dream of becoming creators themselves.
  • Tip: check who is paying for a post and take breaks from the screen.
